Why a Barndominium

A modern answer to how people actually want to live.

Barndominiums combine the large-span flexibility of a barn structure with the comfort and finish of a custom home — open living, oversized garages and workshops, and a strong connection between indoor and outdoor space, all under one roof.

Design Freedom

Open layouts, large spans, custom spaces and architectural flexibility.

Indoor / Outdoor Living

Large openings, covered patios, outdoor kitchens and lifestyle-focused spaces.

Performance

SIP-based construction can provide a high-performance building envelope.

Built Around Your Property

The design conversation begins with your site, not just a stock plan.

One Home. Many Possibilities.

Home, garage, workshop, entertaining, hobbies and family spaces — together.

Construction Approach

Built with performance in mind.

We use Structural Insulated Panel (SIP) systems in our barndominium construction approach, combining structural performance and insulation into a precision-engineered building system.

Energy Performance

A continuous, high-performance building envelope designed to help reduce thermal bridging and air leakage.

Structural Performance

Panels act as a continuous structural system across walls and roof.

Precision

Factory-engineered components help reduce onsite guesswork and waste.
