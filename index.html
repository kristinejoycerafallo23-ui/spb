Construction Approach

Built with performance in mind.

We use Structural Insulated Panel (SIP) systems in our barndominium construction approach, combining structural performance and insulation into a precision-engineered building system.

Energy Performance

A continuous, high-performance building envelope designed to help reduce thermal bridging and air leakage.

Structural Performance

Panels act as a continuous structural system across walls and roof.

Precision

Factory-engineered components help reduce onsite guesswork and waste.

Why SIP Construction?
"We don't build homes layer by layer. We build with a system."
